# Decision Journal

Generates a decision journal note from a template in the 05-Life/Decision-Journal directory.

When invoked, this script creates a new decision journal entry to record important decisions and their reasoning for future learning.

## Usage

Run `/decision-journal` to generate a decision journal note.
You can also provide a title: `/decision-journal Job Offer Decision`
